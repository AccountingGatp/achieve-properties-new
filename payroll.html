4 Download and import

Import steps
  1. Before Wednesday's run: timesheets approved by Veronica / Shanti; payroll is processed Wednesday for same-day funding.
  2. ResMan: Admin → Import. Type Invoices, Property Achieve Properties LLC, choose the ResMan file, Import. Bills land per property using the property code in each line.
  3. QuickBooks (Achieve Properties LLC): Settings → Import data → Invoices → upload the invoice CSV, map fields, import.
  4. QuickBooks JE: Settings → Import data → Journal entries → upload the JE CSV.
  5. Invoice numbers are shared: the QBO invoice and the ResMan bill for a property carry the same number.
  6. Fund payroll: transfer each amount in the Funding table from the property operating account to the payroll account (Achieve's own share from 5632). Transfers over the limit need Shanti's approval. Ask managers for the full amount, not just the shortfall. Attach the QBO invoices to the payroll entry.
  7. Confirm each transfer completed in bank activity — "pending" means it is waiting for James's approval, and the money is not in the payroll account yet. Funds must land by end of Wednesday (earlier in a holiday week).
  8. Short on funds: transfer what the property has, record it as a partial payment in QBO and ResMan, track the balance on the Funding tab and email Shanti for the rest. A property's earlier overpayment sits as payroll escrow (ResMan credit, GL 1206) and can be applied.
  9. Close out each property: in QuickBooks, Receive Payment against the invoice (partial if only part was transferred); in ResMan, mark the bill paid with the EFT method and attach the transfer confirmation.
  10. Attach the QBO invoice PDF to the ResMan bill, and post the QBO payroll JE when ADP's debit hits the payroll account.
  11. Owner's contribution: Shanti's and James's net pay comes back into 5632 — categorize those deposits as Owner's Contribution, not payroll income.
